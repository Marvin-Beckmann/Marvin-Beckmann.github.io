Carsten Baum and I made a follow-up on [PoMFRIT](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/109), and we used ML-DSA instead of MAYO.
We are able to prove possession of an ML-DSA signature (without any modifications) in under 1s and roughly 1 MB.
We also develop new techniques for switching efficiently between different fields in VOLEitH proofs.
Check out our paper [here](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2473.pdf).
