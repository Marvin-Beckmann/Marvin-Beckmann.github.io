Our paper [Concretely Efficient Blind Signatures Based on VOLE-in-the-Head Proofs and the MAYO Trapdoor](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/109) has been accepted at USENIX Security 2026.
We propose PoMFRIT, a post-quantum blind signature scheme that follows the standard Fischlin-style blind signature paradigm where the zero-knowledge proof for MAYO (and SHAKE256) is constructed using VOLEitH.
